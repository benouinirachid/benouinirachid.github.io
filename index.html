About Me

Staff Data Scientist & Researcher

I am a Staff Data Scientist and researcher specializing in Machine Learning, Document Understanding, and Conversational AI. I earned my Ph.D. in Machine Learning (2021), following an M.S. in Intelligent Systems and Networks (2015) and a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics (2013).

I have published over 25 peer-reviewed papers in international journals and conferences, with my research cited more than 400 times. My scientific contributions cover visually-rich document understanding, multimodal learning, pattern recognition, moment invariants, and graph-based models.

At Leyton CognitX, I have led high-impact projects, building production-ready AI solutions that integrate machine learning, multimodal reasoning, compliance automation, and scalable microservices architectures.

My work lies at the intersection of research, innovation, and practice, pushing the boundaries of document AI, conversational agents, and intelligent automation.
